Version: 1.0 (Draft)

# Executive Summary

## Purpose

The Care Ecosystem is a unified digital healthcare platform designed to modernize healthcare operations and improve access to healthcare services across Africa.

It consists of two interconnected products:

- CareHub
- CareFind

Together, these products create a complete healthcare ecosystem connecting healthcare providers with the patients they serve.

---

# Vision

To become Africa's most trusted healthcare technology platform by providing healthcare businesses with world-class operational tools while enabling patients to easily discover, access, and engage healthcare services.

---

# Mission

To digitize healthcare operations through secure, scalable, and intelligent software that improves healthcare delivery, operational efficiency, and patient outcomes.

---

# Ecosystem Overview

The Care Ecosystem is composed of two primary applications.

## CareHub

CareHub is an enterprise Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform used by healthcare businesses to manage daily operations.

Supported healthcare businesses include:

- Pharmacies
- Hospitals
- Clinics
- Laboratories
- Imaging Centres
- Wellness Centres
- Medical Stores

CareHub serves as the operational backbone of the ecosystem.

---

## CareFind

CareFind is the public healthcare discovery platform.

Patients use CareFind to:

- Find healthcare providers
- Search medicines
- Discover laboratories
- Locate hospitals
- Find healthcare professionals
- Book appointments (future)
- Access trusted healthcare information

CareFind is powered by data originating from CareHub.

---

# Relationship Between CareHub and CareFind

CareHub owns healthcare business data.

CareFind consumes approved public data.

Healthcare providers maintain their information within CareHub.

Selected information is published to CareFind based on business settings and visibility rules.

No business information is managed directly within CareFind.

This architecture ensures that healthcare businesses maintain one source of truth while patients always receive current information.

# Engineering Philosophy

The Care Ecosystem is designed as a long-term enterprise software platform.

Every engineering decision must prioritize:

- Security
- Scalability
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Performance
- Developer Experience

Code should solve business problems while remaining understandable by future engineers.

Documentation is considered part of the product.

---

# Product Philosophy

The platform should simplify healthcare operations rather than increase complexity.

Every feature must answer one question:

"Does this improve healthcare delivery?"

If the answer is no, the feature should be reconsidered.

---

# Guiding Principles

The Care Ecosystem is guided by the following principles:

1. Patients come first.

2. Healthcare professionals should spend less time on administration.

3. Businesses should operate more efficiently.

4. Data should exist once and be reused.

5. Security is mandatory.

6. Simplicity beats complexity.

7. Quality is preferred over speed.

8. Every improvement should make the platform easier to maintain.
