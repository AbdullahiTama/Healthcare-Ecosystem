# Project Overview

## What is CareHub?

CareHub is not simply a Pharmacy Management System.

It is an operating system for healthcare businesses.

It allows healthcare providers to manage every aspect of their daily operations through a single platform.

---

## Primary Users

- Pharmacies
- Hospitals
- Clinics
- Laboratories
- Imaging Centers
- Healthcare Administrators

---

## Companion Platform

CareFind is the public-facing application.

Healthcare businesses registered on CareHub may choose to expose selected information publicly through CareFind.

This includes:

- Business profile
- Services
- Medicines
- Opening hours
- Contact information

---

## Long-Term Vision

Become the healthcare infrastructure powering healthcare businesses across Africa.
