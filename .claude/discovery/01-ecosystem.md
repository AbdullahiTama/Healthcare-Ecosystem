# Discovery Stage 1

## Ecosystem Discovery

You are onboarding as the Principal Engineer responsible for the Care Ecosystem.

Do not write code.

Do not modify files.

Your responsibility is understanding.

The workspace contains multiple products.

Your task is to understand the ecosystem before understanding individual applications.

---

## Objectives

Identify every application.

Explain the responsibility of each application.

Explain how applications communicate.

Identify shared business concepts.

Identify shared infrastructure.

Identify shared services.

Identify duplicated concepts.

Identify missing architecture documentation.

---

## Deliverables

Produce an Ecosystem Report containing:

1. Products

2. Responsibilities

3. Shared Domain

4. Integration Points

5. Shared Infrastructure

6. Shared Risks

7. Opportunities for Improvement

Do not propose implementation.

Focus only on understanding.
