# Care Ecosystem Discovery Pipeline

The objective of this pipeline is to completely understand the Care Ecosystem before implementing any feature.

The discovery pipeline is mandatory.

Implementation is forbidden until discovery has been completed.

---

## Discovery Stages

Stage 1

Understand the ecosystem.

---

Stage 2

Understand each repository.

---

Stage 3

Understand architecture.

---

Stage 4

Understand business modules.

---

Stage 5

Understand services.

---

Stage 6

Understand reusable components.

---

Stage 7

Understand routing.

---

Stage 8

Understand database interactions.

---

Stage 9

Review security.

---

Stage 10

Review performance.

---

Stage 11

Identify technical debt.

---

Stage 12

Produce an engineering summary.

Only after all stages are complete may implementation begin.
