# CareHub Glossary

## Patient

A person receiving healthcare services.

---

## Customer

A person purchasing products without becoming a patient.

---

## Encounter

A single interaction between a patient and a healthcare provider.

---

## Consultation

A medical review performed by a doctor.

---

## Prescription

Medication recommended by a healthcare professional.

---

## Dispensing

Supplying prescribed medication to a patient.

---

## Batch

A specific production batch of a medicine.

---

## FEFO

First Expiry First Out.

Medicines closest to expiry should be sold first.

---

## Stock Movement

Any transaction that changes inventory.

Examples:

- Purchase
- Sale
- Return
- Adjustment
- Transfer
