# Product Principles

Every feature added to CareHub must satisfy at least one of these goals.

## Simplicity

Healthcare workers should complete tasks with the fewest clicks possible.

---

## Speed

The system must remain fast even with large datasets.

---

## Reliability

Patient information and inventory data must remain accurate.

---

## Security

Patient information must always be protected.

---

## Scalability

Features should support businesses with:

- One branch
- Ten branches
- Hundreds of branches

without redesigning the architecture.

---

## Reusability

Business logic should be reusable.

Avoid duplicate implementations.

---

## Offline First

Where practical, important workflows should continue functioning with poor internet connectivity.
