# CareHub Project Vision

## Overview

CareHub is a cloud-based Healthcare Management Platform designed to help healthcare providers digitize their daily operations.

Unlike traditional pharmacy software or hospital software, CareHub is designed as a modular Healthcare Operating System.

The platform supports multiple healthcare business types through one unified architecture.

---

## Supported Businesses

- Pharmacies
- Hospitals
- Clinics
- Laboratories
- Imaging Centers
- Medical Stores

---

## Companion Product

CareFind is the public discovery platform connected to CareHub.

Businesses can choose to expose selected information publicly through CareFind.

---

## Core Philosophy

One platform.

Multiple healthcare businesses.

One shared ecosystem.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the default healthcare operating system across Africa.
