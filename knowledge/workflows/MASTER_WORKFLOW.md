↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

Dispensing

↓

Billing

↓

Payment

↓

Discharge

Not every business uses every step.

A pharmacy may only use:

Customer

↓

Prescription

↓

Dispensing

↓

Payment

A laboratory may use:

Patient

↓

Sample Collection

↓

Testing

↓

Result

↓

Payment
